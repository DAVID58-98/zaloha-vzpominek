What the tapes are played on

With old tape, the equipment matters more than anything else. For VHS and VHS-C we use a Panasonic NV-HS1000 video recorder with TBC (Time Base Correction), which straightens out the signal's time base and stops the picture jittering and waving. The analogue signal then passes through a professional Canopus ADVC 300converter, also with TBC. That is what keeps the picture from drifting out of sync with the sound, which is the usual failing of cheap USB grabbers.

Small VHS-C cassettes from family camcorders go into a mechanical adapter and play through the same chain, so you need neither your own camera nor an adapter. There is more on this in our article on digitising VHS-C tapes, and a comparison with the DIY approach in our article on cheap USB capture devices.

How the whole thing works

  1. You get in touch. By phone, by email or through the form. We establish how many tapes you have and what type they are.
  2. You hand the tapes over. In person in Prague 4 – Krč by arrangement, or send them via Balíkovna. You receive a Handover Receipt stating the turnaround.
  3. The tapes are digitised. The transfer runs in real time, so an hour of footage means an hour of work. Basic picture and sound treatment is included.
  4. You check the result and pay. Only then. Your tapes come back to you as they were.

We keep a backup copy for 12 months in case you lose the USB drive, then delete it permanently. We do not pass the content of your tapes to anyone.

Damaged and mouldy tapes

Do not throw away a snapped, chewed-up or mouldy tape, and above all do not try to play it in a home video recorder. Splicing and cleaning is a separate service at 300 CZK per tape. Never repair tape with sticky tape – the adhesive spreads inside the mechanism and ruins both the cassette and the player. More in our article on repairing damaged tapes.

Not just VHS?

Besides classic VHS and VHS-C we also digitise Hi8 and Video8 from Sony Handycam camcorders, Mini DV and old DVD discs. The hourly rate is the same for every format, so you are welcome to bring a mixed box of tapes all at once.
