Digitising VHS as an original gift – give someone their memories

Every year brings the same dilemma. What to buy your parents for Christmas? How to mark a grandparent's big birthday? Usually you hear: „Don't buy anything, we've got everything.“ And they are right – material things lose their appeal. What they do not have, and what they miss, is a way back to when they were young.

You have a rare chance to give them a time machine. Digitising old tapes is not just a technical process. It is a way of living again through moments that seemed lost for good in the static of magnetic tape. Záloha vzpomínek is glad to help you with it.

A gift that brings happy tears

Picture the scene. Presents are being opened, paper rustling. Someone you love finds a small box holding a neat USB flash drive. They may be puzzled for a moment. Then you plug it into the television – and a grainy but vivid picture from 1995 appears on the screen.

These are not just videos, they are feelings. A gift like this works as a bridge between generations:

  • For grandparents: a reminder of when they were in their prime and their children were small.
  • For parents: a nostalgic look back at the start of their life together.
  • For grandchildren: entertaining and educational viewing („Dad, you had hair?!“).

A gift like this often outdoes the most expensive material presents, because it goes straight to the heart.

How to set up the surprise

There are two ways to pull this original gift off:

  1. The surprise route: if you can get at the tapes, quietly borrow them. Bring them to us or send them by post (see the article on sending tapes by post). We handle the professional transfer of VHS, VHS-C or Hi8 to USB, and you wrap the drive in a gift box.
  2. The gift voucher route: not sure which tapes are the right ones? Use our gift vouchers worth 500, 1,000 or 1,500 CZK, printed or digital, which makes them ideal at the last minute. The recipient chooses which tapes to rescue.

A shared experience that brings people together

The real magic of digitising video tapes is what happens after the unwrapping. The whole family sits down in front of the television. Instead of staring at phones, you share memories and tell stories that would otherwise stay forgotten.

Video from VHS on USB becomes an interactive experience. And because it is digital, you can easily send it to relatives wherever they live.

🎁

A last-minute gift tip: we email digital gift vouchers, so you can still give one even if you only think of it on Christmas Eve.

Do not leave it for later

Tapes age and the quality gets worse every day. Do not leave this gift until the tape can no longer be read, or until the people on it are no longer with us.

Make someone happy, and yourself too. Wondering about timing so it is ready for the party or for Christmas? Work out the price in the calculator, look through our frequently asked questions and write to us through the contact form. Záloha vzpomínek is the loveliest way of saying „I love you and I value the time we have spent together“.
